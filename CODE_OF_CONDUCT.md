# Code of Conduct

> **DRAFT:** Proposed code. No enforcement committee exists yet; the interim process below applies until formal governance is established.

## Our pledge

IA Para Todos is dedicated to providing a welcoming, safe, and harassment-free experience for everyone — regardless of age, language, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, education level, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community, online and at in-person workshops.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness, especially toward beginners.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for learners and the community.
- Using welcoming, inclusive language in Spanish and English.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Pressuring participants about religious affiliation, immigration status, or other sensitive personal matters.
- Proselytizing, commercial solicitation, or political campaigning at educational events.
- Disrupting workshops or sabotaging others' learning.
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in an educational setting.

## Scope

This Code applies in all community spaces (repository, issues, discussions, events) and at all IA Para Todos workshops, including those hosted at partner venues.

## Enforcement (interim process)

Because no formal committee exists yet:

- **At events:** report to the lead facilitator or any volunteer on site. Facilitators prioritize the safety of the affected person first.
- **Online:** report to a repository maintainer through GitHub (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for private-reporting guidance). Do not post private reports as public issues.
- All reports are handled confidentially and reviewed promptly and fairly.
- Consequences scale with severity: private correction → warning with conditions → temporary removal from events/spaces → permanent ban.
- Maintainers and facilitators who fail to uphold this Code in good faith face the same consequences.
