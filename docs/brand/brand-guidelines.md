# Brand Guidelines (Provisional)

> **DRAFT:** Provisional foundation only. No finalized logo, trademark, color palette, or registered domain exists — nothing here claims otherwise. Revisit with community input after the pilot.

## Name

**IA Para Todos** — always written this way: "IA" (not "Ia" or "I.A."), "Para Todos" capitalized. English gloss on first use in English contexts: "IA Para Todos (AI for Everyone)."

## Working tagline

> "Inteligencia Artificial para Todos. Un Futuro Sin Barreras."
> English gloss: "AI for Everyone. A Future Without Barriers."

Working tagline, not a registered mark. Use it on outreach, handouts, and the repository header; don't carve it in stone until the community has lived with it.

## Brand attributes

- **Welcoming / Acogedor** — a first-time learner feels invited, never tested.
- **Bilingual / Bilingüe** — Spanish-first, English-supported; both treated with equal care.
- **Accessible / Accesible** — plain language, readable formats, no intimidation.
- **Trustworthy / Confiable** — honest limits, no hype, no hidden motives, privacy kept.
- **Educational / Educativo** — teaching is the product; everything else serves it.
- **Community-centered / Comunitario** — the community's needs and venues lead; we follow.
- **Modern without being intimidating / Moderno sin intimidar** — current tools, patient tone.

## Audience fit

One brand must work for churches, schools, libraries, volunteers, sponsors, and the general public. Rules that keep it universal:

- Nonsectarian visuals and language everywhere, regardless of venue.
- Non-commercial: no vendor logos, no sponsor marks inside educational materials (acknowledgments live on a separate line/page, if ever used).
- Dignified and warm: depict community members as capable learners, never as helpless or behind.
- Spanish leads on participant materials; organizational materials may lead in either language.

## Provisional visual direction (not final)

- **Logo:** none yet. When needed, use the wordmark "IA Para Todos" in clean type. Do not commission or adopt a mark until post-pilot.
- **Colors:** no palette selected. Interim: high-contrast, print-friendly (black text, white background, one accent used sparingly) for readability and cheap printing.
- **Typography:** system fonts only for now (readable, free, no licensing); minimum 14pt on print.
- **Photography/illustration:** no participant photos by default; if imagery is ever used, consent-first, dignified, representative of the community — and never committed with identifying details.

> **DECISION NEEDED:** logo, palette, typography, domain, and trademark review — all deferred until after pilot validation.

## Usage rules

- Don't alter the name or tagline wording; don't translate the name itself ("AI for Everyone" is a gloss, not the name).
- Don't place the brand on materials promising jobs, certification, or outcomes we don't offer.
- Don't co-brand with vendors or imply endorsements in either direction.
- Do keep every public claim consistent with [messaging.md](messaging.md) and the true project status.
