# Messaging

> **DRAFT:** Provisional key messages and boilerplate. Use verbatim where marked ✅; adapt the rest to context without changing facts or status.

## Boilerplate (✅ use verbatim, fill nothing in)

**Español:**
> IA Para Todos es una iniciativa comunitaria que ofrece talleres gratuitos en español para aprender a usar la inteligencia artificial en la vida diaria, el trabajo y la familia. Nuestras sesiones son para principiantes, solo requieren un teléfono inteligente y no tienen ningún costo.

**English:**
> IA Para Todos (AI for Everyone) is a community initiative offering free Spanish-language workshops that help adults understand and use artificial intelligence in daily life, work, and family. Sessions are beginner-friendly, smartphone-only, and always free.

## Status line (✅ append whenever describing the project publicly)

**Español:**
> Estamos en etapa exploratoria: aún no realizamos talleres oficiales ni formamos alianzas formales, y no somos una organización sin fines de lucro registrada.

**English:**
> We are in the exploratory stage: we have not yet held official workshops or formed formal partnerships, and we are not a registered nonprofit.

## Key messages

1. **Free and for beginners** — no cost, no experience, no paid tools, no laptop needed.
2. **Spanish-first, community-rooted** — taught in the community's language, at trusted local venues, starting in Dallas–Fort Worth.
3. **Practical, not theoretical** — every participant completes a real task before leaving.
4. **Safe and honest** — fictional practice data, privacy protection, and explicit teaching on AI limits and verification.
5. **Inclusive and nonsectarian** — open to everyone; no religious content or affiliation required.
6. **Evidence-seeking** — we're validating with small pilots and publishing what we learn, including misses.

## What we never claim

- Nonprofit / 501(c)(3) / charity / tax-exempt status (none exists).
- Partnerships, sponsors, or endorsements (none confirmed).
- Results, statistics, or testimonials (no workshops held; targets are labeled experimental).
- Jobs, certification, accreditation, income, or client outcomes.
- That AI is safe, neutral, or always correct — or that avoiding it entirely is the answer.
