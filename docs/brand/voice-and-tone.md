# Voice and Tone

> **DRAFT:** Provisional guide. Apply to all outreach, curricula, and public docs; refine with community feedback after the pilot.

## Voice (who we are, always)

- **Warm and patient** — like a trusted neighbor who happens to know technology, not a lecturer or a salesperson.
- **Clear and plain** — short sentences, everyday words, every technical term defined on first use.
- **Honest about limits** — we say what AI can't do and what we don't know. Uncertainty is stated, not hidden.
- **Encouraging without hype** — celebrate real small wins; never promise transformation, income, or jobs.
- **Respectful of the audience** — adults with full lives and real expertise; beginners at this topic, not beginners at life.

## Tone (how we adjust by situation)

| Situation | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Workshop instruction | Patient, step-by-step, celebratory | "Vamos paso a paso. ¿Listos? Toca el cuadro de texto… ¡muy bien!" |
| Outreach to hosts | Respectful, concrete, honest about stage | "Estamos validando el programa con grupos pequeños antes de crecer." |
| Privacy and safety | Firm, caring, unambiguous | "Nunca escribas datos reales. Practicamos con ejemplos inventados." |
| AI limitations | Direct, hype-free | "La IA a veces inventa respuestas con total seguridad. Por eso verificamos." |
| Feedback and failure | Humble, grateful, specific | "Solo 6 de 14 completaron el ejercicio. Esto es lo que cambiaremos." |
| Funders/sponsors (future) | Professional, evidence-based, boundaried | "Estos son nuestros resultados agregados y nuestros costos reales." |

## Spanish-first rules

- Participant-facing content is written in Spanish first — natural, conversational, tested with community readers — then English where needed.
- Never machine-translate blindly: a bilingual human reviews every translation.
- Prefer "tú" (warm, direct) in workshop materials; "usted" only where a host community prefers formality — ask, don't assume.
- English gloss in parentheses on first use of key terms where helpful (e.g., "retroalimentación (feedback)").

## Words we use and avoid

| Use | Avoid | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Taller gratuito para principiantes" | "Capacitación / masterclass / bootcamp" | Intimidating or implies payment/level |
| "Practicar / probar" | "Dominar / explotar la IA" | Hype; pressure |
| "La IA a veces se equivoca" | "La IA es peligrosa / es perfecta" | Fear or blind trust |
| "Verificar en otro lado" | "Validar fuentes" (unexplained) | Jargon |
| "Comunidad / vecinos" | "Usuarios / beneficiarios" | Cold; transactional |
| "Aún estamos aprendiendo" | "Somos líderes / expertos #1" | False authority |
