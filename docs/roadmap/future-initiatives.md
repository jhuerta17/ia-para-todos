# Future Initiatives

> **DRAFT:** Idea backlog only. **None of these are approved programs.** Each would need its own validation, curriculum, safeguarding review, and milestone gate after the core series proves repeatable (see [milestones.md](milestones.md) M4+).

## Candidate ideas (unranked, unapproved)

- **Digital literacy basics** — device operation, accounts, passwords, safe browsing; a formalized version of the proposed pre-Workshop-1 orientation.
- **AI for small businesses** — deeper track spun out of Workshop 3: pricing, customer follow-up, simple bookkeeping assistance, online presence.
- **Workforce development** — multi-session cohorts with employer or agency partners: job search, workplace AI tools, interview practice, digital portfolios.
- **Parent and family AI literacy** — extended series from Workshop 4: school platforms, parental controls, ongoing scam-defense updates as patterns evolve.
- **Cybersecurity awareness** — passwords, phishing, safe Wi-Fi, device updates, identity protection; complementary to every track.
- **Student technology workshops** — youth-facing, only under full [safeguarding](../safety/participant-safeguarding.md) compliance: consent, vetting, supervision, school policies. Highest bar, last priority.
- **Volunteer instructor training** — structured facilitator cohorts with certification-of-completion (not accreditation), practice teaching, and mentoring.
- **Community technology events** — demo days, Q&A forums, "bring your phone" help desks at partner venues; low-commitment on-ramps to workshops.

## Promotion criteria (any idea → approved program)

1. Core series repeatability proven (M4) with spare volunteer capacity.
2. Documented community request (not assumed) from interviews or feedback.
3. Draft curriculum exists and passes responsible-AI and privacy review.
4. Safeguarding requirements met (especially for youth — non-negotiable).
5. Venue type validated and costs known from a small test.
6. No funding strings attached that distort the mission.

Ideas failing any criterion stay ideas. The backlog growing is fine; the program growing without evidence is not.

## Explicitly not planned

- Certification or accreditation programs; job placement services; paid courses.
- Policy lobbying; vendor partnerships with content influence; franchising the brand.
- Anything requiring participant data collection beyond anonymous aggregates.
