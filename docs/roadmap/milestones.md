# Milestones

> **DRAFT:** Proposed conditional milestones. Nothing approved, scheduled, or started. Each milestone unlocks only when the previous one's evidence exists.

## How to read this roadmap

Milestones are **gates, not dates**. A phase begins when its entry criteria are met — whenever that is — and the project may legitimately stop or pause at any gate. "Stop" is a successful outcome if the evidence says stop.

## M0 — Repository and drafts complete (this setup, Parts 1–7)

- Entry: project exploration begins.
- Exit: all seven setup phases landed; every doc honest about draft status; zero dead links; zero invented facts.
- Status: complete (all seven setup phases landed).

## M1 — Validation complete (90-day pilot, Days 1–30)

- Entry: M0 done; founder time reserved for interviews and outreach.
- Exit: ~10 participant interviews documented; 3–5 host conversations held; ≥1 venue secured; Workshop 1 rehearsed.
- Evidence: anonymized needs summary, venue assessment (private), rehearsal notes.

## M2 — Pilot delivered (Days 31–60)

- Entry: M1 exit criteria met.
- Exit: ~2 workshops delivered to 10–20 participants each; ≥1 assistant facilitator onboarded; feedback and costs recorded.
- Evidence: attendance counts, completion/confidence tallies, cost log, volunteer debrief.

## M3 — Pilot evaluated (Days 61–90)

- Entry: M2 evidence in hand.
- Exit: written verdict (repeat / adjust / pause); revised Workshop 1; known cost per participant; formation recommendation.
- Evidence: [pilot retrospective](../../templates/pilot-retrospective.md) (anonymized), revised curriculum, demand assessment.

## M4 — Repeatability proven (proposed, post-pilot)

- Entry: M3 verdict is "repeat," with funding for marginal costs.
- Exit: second venue delivers Workshop 1; a **non-founder** lead facilitator succeeds using only written materials; demand sustains without extraordinary effort.
- Evidence: second-venue retrospective, facilitator pipeline count (≥2 leads).

## M5 — Series running (proposed)

- Entry: M4 repeatability demonstrated.
- Exit: full four-workshop series cycling; libraries/centers added as venue types; volunteer bench covers scheduling without founder presence.
- Evidence: series completion data, per-venue retrospectives, volunteer roster (private).

## M6 — Formal structure decided (proposed)

- Entry: M5 running; funding needs exceed out-of-pocket sustainability.
- Exit: fiscal sponsorship secured **or** independent formation deliberately chosen with board and funding — documented with rationale.
- Evidence: signed sponsorship agreement or filed formation documents (verified live, not from memory).

## M7 — Sustained operation (proposed)

- Entry: M6 structure in place.
- Exit: quarterly cadence of workshops, published aggregate impact, funded costs, governed finances, succession-tested (founder absence doesn't stop delivery).
- Evidence: public impact summaries, financial reports, board minutes (private).
