# 90-Day Pilot Plan

> **DRAFT:** Proposed plan, not a commitment. No phase has started; day counts begin whenever validation work actually begins. All targets require validation.

## Purpose

Answer three questions with evidence before anything else: **Is there real demand? Can beginners actually learn this way? What does it really cost?** The pilot is a test with go/no-go criteria, not a launch event.

## Days 1–30: Validate

**Goal:** confirm audience needs and secure the conditions for a fair test.

- [ ] Contact 3–5 potential community partner organizations (see [outreach-process.md](../operations/outreach-process.md)).
- [ ] Interview ~10 prospective participants about needs, availability, devices, and skepticism.
- [ ] Validate educational needs: which topics matter, what level people start from, what would make them show up.
- [ ] Prepare the introductory curriculum (Workshop 1) to pilot-ready state from the [draft](../../workshops/01-introduccion-ia/).
- [ ] Secure a potential venue: walkthrough complete ([venue-assessment](../../templates/venue-assessment.md)), responsibility split agreed.
- [ ] Assess equipment and accessibility needs (hotspot? large print? seating? transport?).

**Exit criteria:** ≥1 confirmed venue, documented audience needs from ~10 interviews, Workshop 1 rehearsed end-to-end. If no venue or no evidenced interest: pause and rethink rather than forcing Days 31–60.

## Days 31–60: Pilot

**Goal:** deliver small, observe honestly, support every learner.

- [ ] Conduct ~2 pilot workshops (Workshop 1), targeting 10–20 participants each.
- [ ] Recruit and onboard at least one assistant facilitator (see [volunteer-onboarding.md](../operations/volunteer-onboarding.md)).
- [ ] Document all costs with receipts ([budget-template.md](../operations/budget-template.md)).
- [ ] Collect anonymous feedback at each session ([workshop-feedback](../../templates/workshop-feedback.md)).
- [ ] Measure practical outcomes: exercises completed, confidence before/after (see [impact-measurement.md](../operations/impact-measurement.md)).
- [ ] Debrief volunteers and host after each session; record observations.

**Exit criteria:** 2 sessions delivered (or a documented reason they weren't), feedback tallied, costs logged, retrospective drafted. Small turnout is data, not failure — record causes.

## Days 61–90: Evaluate

**Goal:** decide what the evidence supports — repeat, adjust, or stop.

- [ ] Improve the curriculum from pilot evidence (file each change with its motivating observation).
- [ ] Evaluate demand: attendance vs. outreach effort, host enthusiasm vs. follow-through, repeat requests.
- [ ] Test another venue if feasible (validates portability, not just one friendly room).
- [ ] Determine actual operating costs: cost per workshop and per participant from receipts.
- [ ] Identify potential volunteers (facilitator pipeline) and prospective board members (relationships only — no entity exists).
- [ ] Evaluate fiscal sponsorship vs. independent formation in light of real costs and demand (see [formation-options.md](../nonprofit/formation-options.md)).

**Exit criteria:** written pilot verdict (repeat / adjust / pause), revised Workshop 1, known costs, documented demand assessment, and a formation recommendation with rationale.

## Experimental targets (proposed, unvalidated)

From [impact-measurement.md](../operations/impact-measurement.md); repeated here for convenience:

- 75% registration-to-attendance rate (if registration is used).
- 80% of participants complete at least one practical task.
- 80% report improved confidence.
- 50% report using AI again after 30 days.

Missing a target triggers curriculum or process changes — not spin. Report misses alongside hits.

## Non-goals for the 90 days

- No second workshop series launch; no youth programming; no fundraising campaign.
- No legal entity formation; no multi-venue expansion; no paid staff.
- No public claims beyond "we piloted, here's what we learned."
