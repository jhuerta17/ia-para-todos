# Target Audience

> **DRAFT:** Initial audience hypotheses. To be validated through interviews and pilot workshops before any formalization.

## Primary audience (proposed)

**Spanish-speaking adults unfamiliar with artificial intelligence**, beginning with Hispanic and Mexican-American communities in the Dallas–Fort Worth area.

### Audience segments

- **Parents / Padres y madres** — trying to understand technologies their children increasingly use at school and at home.
- **Working adults / Adultos trabajadores** — seeking productivity improvements and keeping up with workplace change.
- **Small-business owners and entrepreneurs / Dueños de pequeños negocios y emprendedores** — exploring AI for communication, marketing, and organization.
- **Older adults / Adultos mayores** — learning new digital tools, often starting from lower baseline digital literacy.
- **Community members with limited digital literacy** — who may need help with devices, accounts, and internet basics before AI topics are reachable.
- **Individuals seeking employment and career development** — résumés, interview preparation, job search.

## Do not assume — design for variance

Do **not** assume participants have:

- Laptops (design smartphone-first).
- Email accounts (offer alternatives when account creation isn't practical).
- Strong or reliable internet access.
- Advanced — or even basic — digital skills.
- The same education level, literacy, English proficiency, or learning goals.

Concretely: include readiness checks, an optional digital-skills orientation, step-by-step instructions, and assistant facilitators who can help strugglers without stopping the group. See [../strategy/risks-and-mitigation.md](../strategy/risks-and-mitigation.md) (digital literacy barriers, unreliable internet/equipment).

## Future audiences (out of pilot scope)

- **Students and younger audiences** — a possible future program area, subject to appropriate safeguarding, age restrictions, supervision, and school requirements.
- **English-speaking beginners** — materials designed to be translatable; English delivery is a future option.
- **Workforce-development cohorts** — structured multi-session tracks with employer or agency partners.

## Non-audiences (by design)

- Advanced or technical learners seeking deep AI training — other programs serve them; our materials stay introductory.
- Anyone expecting certification, accreditation, or job placement — we do not offer these, and outreach must never imply them.
