# Organizational Overview

> **DRAFT:** Working document for an early-stage initiative. Describes aspirations and plans, not accomplishments.

## What IA Para Todos aspires to be

IA Para Todos ("AI for Everyone") is a community education initiative intended to reduce the digital divide through **free, accessible, practical, beginner-friendly workshops** on artificial intelligence and emerging technologies.

- **Tagline:** "Inteligencia Artificial para Todos. Un Futuro Sin Barreras."
- **Intended audience:** Spanish-speaking adults with limited exposure to AI, beginning with Mexican-American and other Hispanic communities in Dallas–Fort Worth, Texas.
- **Intended starting venues:** Local Hispanic churches as pilot partners, expanding over time to libraries, community centers, schools, nonprofits, workforce programs, and other accessible venues.
- **Character:** Inclusive and nonsectarian. Participation would never depend on religious affiliation or background.

## What this initiative is and is not

| It IS intended to be | It is NOT |
|---|---|
| Free beginner AI/digital-literacy workshops | A coding bootcamp or technical training program |
| Smartphone-first, no paid tools required | A product endorsement or AI vendor channel |
| Community-partnered and volunteer-powered | A lead-generation funnel for any business |
| Evidence-seeking (pilot → measure → improve) | A proven, scaled program |
| Inclusive and nonsectarian | Affiliated with any church, party, or company |
