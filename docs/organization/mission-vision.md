# Mission and Vision

> **DRAFT:** Initial working drafts, not finalized or board-approved statements. There is no board yet; these will be revisited after pilot validation and during any future formalization.

## Mission

**Español:**

> "Reducir la brecha digital mediante talleres gratuitos, accesibles y prácticos que ayuden a las comunidades a comprender y aprovechar la inteligencia artificial y las nuevas tecnologías."

**English:**

> "To reduce the digital divide through free, accessible, and practical workshops that empower communities to understand and use artificial intelligence and emerging technologies."

### What the mission means in practice

- **Free** — no charge to participants, no paid tools required.
- **Accessible** — beginner-friendly, smartphone-first, in the community's language, at trusted local venues.
- **Practical** — every session ends with something the participant can actually do, not just theory.
- **Empower** — learners leave more capable and more critical, not dependent on any tool or instructor.

## Vision

**Español:**

> "Un futuro donde todas las personas, sin importar su idioma, edad o experiencia tecnológica, tengan la oportunidad de aprender, adaptarse y beneficiarse de la inteligencia artificial."

**English:**

> "A future where everyone, regardless of language, age, or technological experience, has the opportunity to learn, adapt, and benefit from artificial intelligence."

### What the vision points toward

- Language, age, and prior experience are never barriers to learning.
- Communities adapt to technological change on their own terms.
- The benefits of AI reach underserved communities, not just early adopters.

## How to use these statements

- Use them in outreach and partnership conversations to describe **intent**.
- Do not present them as approved by a board or legal entity — no such entity exists.
- Revisions should incorporate community input and, once formed, governance approval.
