# Core Values

> **DRAFT:** Working values for an early-stage initiative. All public-facing documents should reinforce them.

1. **Accessibility / Accesibilidad** — Meet learners where they are: their language, their devices (usually smartphones), their venues, their schedules. Remove every avoidable barrier to showing up and learning.

2. **Inclusion / Inclusión** — Everyone is welcome regardless of language, age, education, background, or beliefs. Program content is nonsectarian and culturally respectful. Never assume all Spanish-speaking participants share the same needs.

3. **Community empowerment / Empoderamiento comunitario** — Build lasting capability in communities, not dependence on us. Teach people to learn, verify, and keep going after the workshop ends.

4. **Lifelong learning / Aprendizaje permanente** — Learning has no age limit and no finish line. We celebrate beginners and design for people returning to learning after years away.

5. **Responsible AI / IA responsable** — Teach what AI can and cannot do, its limits, its biases, and its risks. Honest, hype-free education in every session.

6. **Privacy / Privacidad** — Protect learners' data and teach them to protect their own. Never use real sensitive information in exercises; never store participant data in this public repository.

7. **Digital safety / Seguridad digital** — Equip participants to navigate online risks: scams, misinformation, oversharing, and unsafe defaults. Safety guidance belongs in every workshop, not in a separate optional module.

8. **Collaboration / Colaboración** — Partner with trusted community organizations; recruit, train, and respect volunteers. Shared ownership beats founder dependency.

9. **Transparency / Transparencia** — Work in the open, measure honestly (including failures), report costs and results, and correct course in public. Proposed targets are experimental, never marketing claims.

## Using these values

- **Curriculum decisions:** if a proposed exercise conflicts with a value (e.g., requires a paid tool — violates Accessibility), change the exercise.
- **Partnership decisions:** partners should align with Inclusion and Transparency.
- **Conflict resolution:** when values appear to conflict, prioritize participant safety and privacy first, then accessibility and inclusion.
