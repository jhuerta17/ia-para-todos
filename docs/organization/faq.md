# Frequently Asked Questions

> **DRAFT:** Answers reflect current working positions. Update as decisions are made. No partnerships, statistics, or accomplishments below are real yet — where something is planned rather than done, it says so.

## General

**What is IA Para Todos? / ¿Qué es IA Para Todos?**
A community education initiative with a bilingual website, student portal, reservations, and Meta Muse practice, supporting free beginner workshops. Published future sessions are not evidence of delivered workshops. See [current readiness](../readiness.md).

**Is it a nonprofit? / ¿Es una organización sin fines de lucro?**
Not currently. It is an early-stage initiative that may explore options such as fiscal sponsorship or forming an independent nonprofit in the future. No legal entity exists today.

**Is it free? / ¿Es gratuito?**
The plan is yes: workshops would be free for participants, using only free tools. Participants would need only a smartphone.

**Is it religious? / ¿Es religioso?**
No. IA Para Todos is intended to be inclusive and nonsectarian. Churches may serve as initial host venues because they are trusted community spaces, but participation would never depend on religious affiliation, and workshops would contain no religious content.

**Do I need a laptop or prior experience? / ¿Necesito una computadora o experiencia previa?**
No. Workshops are being designed for smartphones and absolute beginners, with an optional digital-skills orientation for participants who need device basics first.

## Workshops and reservations

**What would I learn? / ¿Qué aprendería?**
Depending on the session: what AI is and isn't, drafting messages, translating information, organizing tasks, professional communication, résumé help — and, for parents, how children use AI plus online safety. Every session would cover AI accuracy, privacy, and how to verify information independently.

**How long would workshops be?**
Curricula propose 90–120 minutes, including hands-on practice. Published sessions can differ; check the live agenda for the actual start/end time.

**Would my data be collected? / ¿Se recopilarían mis datos?**
Workshop attendance and feedback use anonymous aggregates. Optional reservations
store a preferred name and email or phone privately. Student accounts use Identity;
progress and saved practice use private PostgreSQL. Learning/practice has separate
Meta Contributor consent. No participant records belong in the public repository.
Use fictional exercises. See [privacy guidelines](../safety/privacy-guidelines.md).

## Student accounts

**How can judges register? / ¿Cómo se registran los jueces?**
Use [student signup](https://ia-para-todos-jh.netlify.app/portal/sign-up), your own
email, an eight-character-or-longer password, and the adult/privacy acknowledgement.
Confirm by email. Registration must be open and pilot capacity available. Password
recovery is on [sign-in](https://ia-para-todos-jh.netlify.app/portal/sign-in).
Courses/saved practice require additional Contributor consent; basic registration
covers account/reservation/preparation storage only. See [student operations](../operations/student-portal.md).

## Partners and volunteers (planned)

**How could my organization host a workshop?**
Host outreach is planned for a later phase. During validation we intend to work with a small number of hosts before anything is repeated or expanded. No partnerships exist today.

**How could I volunteer?**
Volunteer roles (facilitators, assistants, translators, outreach helpers) will be defined in a later phase. No volunteers have been recruited yet.

**Can I use these materials?**
Proposed dual licensing — CC BY 4.0 for educational materials, MIT for scripts — is documented in [LICENSE](../../LICENSE) and is pending final approval. Until approved, treat reuse conservatively and ask the maintainers if in doubt.

## Trust and safety

**Are you selling something or promoting an AI product?**
No. The initiative is intended to stay independent from any commercial activity, endorse no AI vendor, and never be used for lead generation. See [../strategy/risks-and-mitigation.md](../strategy/risks-and-mitigation.md) (conflicts of interest).

**How would you handle AI mistakes and misinformation?**
By teaching AI limitations explicitly, practicing fact-checking in every workshop, and instructing participants to verify important information independently. See [../strategy/risks-and-mitigation.md](../strategy/risks-and-mitigation.md) (AI misinformation).
