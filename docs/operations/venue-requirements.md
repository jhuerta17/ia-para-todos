# Venue Requirements

> **DRAFT:** Proposed requirements. Validate and adjust after the first venue walkthroughs.

What a workshop venue needs, and who provides what. Written to be shared directly with prospective hosts.

## Space and capacity

- **Seating for the expected group plus 20%** (pilot: plan 12–24 seats for 10–20 participants), arranged so people can work in pairs.
- Tables or flat surfaces for phones and printed sheets (preferred; note if unavailable so materials adapt).
- Accessible entrance, seating, and restrooms for people with limited mobility.
- Adequate lighting (participants read phone screens and printouts) and ventilation.

## Technical requirements

| Need | Minimum | Preferred |
|---|---|---|
| Internet | None if offline alternatives confirmed — but test first | Reliable Wi-Fi reaching all seats |
| Display | Large TV or blank wall for projection | Projector + screen, tested with our adapter |
| Audio | Facilitator voice projection for the room size | Small speaker for demo audio |
| Power | Enough outlets for facilitator kit | Outlets or strips reachable from seats |
| Phones | Participants' own smartphones (any recent model) | Plus 1–2 spare demo phones we bring |

**Smartphone compatibility:** all exercises work in a phone browser with free tools. No laptops, no app installs required, no paid accounts. If the venue has no usable internet, we bring a hotspot and paper-based backup activities.

## Language accessibility

- Sessions are delivered in Spanish; printed materials are Spanish-first.
- Host announcements should go out in Spanish (we provide sample text on request).
- If the host community needs bilingual (ES/EN) support, confirm in advance so we staff accordingly.

## Safety and emergency procedures

- Identify before the session: emergency exits, first-aid kit location, and the on-site safety contact (name + role, kept private — never committed to this repo).
- Agree who holds liability/insurance coverage for the event (host's policy, organizer's policy, or acknowledged gap with a plan to close it).
- Keep a clear path to exits; do not overload power strips; tape down cables.
- In case of emergency: stop the session, follow the venue's procedures, account for volunteers.

## Division of responsibilities

| Item | Host organization | IA Para Todos team |
|---|---|---|
| Space, seating, restrooms, basic accessibility | Provides | Verifies in walkthrough |
| Internet (if available) and power access | Provides info/access | Brings hotspot backup |
| Projector/screen or large display | Provides if available | Brings adapters; confirms compatibility |
| Community outreach and announcements | Leads (their channels) | Provides sample text |
| On-site safety contact and emergency info | Designates | Follows and documents privately |
| Insurance/liability clarity | Discloses their coverage | Discloses ours; records the agreement |
| Facilitators, materials, printed handouts | — | Provides |
| Anonymous feedback collection | — | Provides and safeguards |

Record the agreed split per event (a simple email confirmation suffices) and keep it outside this public repository.
