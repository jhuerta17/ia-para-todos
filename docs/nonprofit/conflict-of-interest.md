# Conflict of Interest

> **DRAFT — INFORMATIONAL, NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Proposed principles for a future formal policy. No policy adopted, no board exists to adopt one. Have a qualified attorney review any policy before adoption.

## Purpose

Protect the initiative's independence and community trust — particularly because the founder has separate professional and commercial activities in technology. Education must never become marketing.

## Core rule

**IA Para Todos must never become a commercial lead-generation channel.** No attendee access, contact lists, endorsements, or program content may be used to benefit any business — the founder's or anyone else's. This rule applies under every operating model, starting today.

## What counts as a conflict

A conflict (or its appearance) arises when a decision-maker's personal, family, or business interests could influence — or appear to influence — an initiative decision. Examples:

- Selecting tools, vendors, or venues connected to a decision-maker.
- Hiring, paying, or contracting with related persons or businesses.
- Using participant or partner relationships for commercial purposes.
- Endorsing a product or service in curricula or outreach.
- A board member's (future) business transacting with the initiative.

## Required safeguards

1. **Disclosure:** decision-makers disclose relevant interests when joining and annually, plus promptly when new conflicts arise. Disclosures are recorded privately.
2. **Recusal:** conflicted individuals leave the room (physically or virtually) for discussion and abstain from voting on the matter.
3. **Independent review:** any transaction involving a related party requires evaluation and approval by disinterested parties, documented with comparability evidence (independent quotes or market data).
4. **No single control:** no conflicted individual may unilaterally approve, sign, or execute a related-party matter.
5. **Curriculum neutrality:** workshop materials recommend categories of free tools, never a single vendor, and never a related business. No sponsor or partner may dictate content.
6. **Data separation:** initiative contacts, attendees, and partner relationships are never shared with, sold to, or used by any business.

## Related-party transactions (future)

If the initiative ever pays, contracts, or transacts with a business connected to a decision-maker, all of the following are required: advance written disclosure; demonstrated fair value; approval by disinterested decision-makers; written recusal record; and periodic re-review. When in doubt, the transaction does not proceed.

## Violations

Suspected violations are reported to the lead organizer (now) or board chair (later), reviewed confidentially, and documented. Remedies range from process correction to ending the transaction or the individual's role. Retaliation against good-faith reporters is prohibited.

## Privacy note

This repository contains no names, financial information, or details about any founder businesses — by design, permanently. Disclosures, when they exist, are private records, never committed here.
