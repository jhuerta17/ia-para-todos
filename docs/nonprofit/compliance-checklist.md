## If independence is ever pursued — verification checklist

- [ ] **Texas nonprofit incorporation:** current certificate of formation requirements, required provisions and purpose language, registered agent rules, and filing method. Source: Texas Secretary of State.
- [ ] **Formation costs and processing times:** verify live; never budget from memory or third-party blogs.
- [ ] **Federal tax exemption:** which application form fits (full vs. streamlined), eligibility conditions, user fees, and current processing realities. Source: IRS.
- [ ] **Federal annual filing obligations:** which annual return/information filing applies at which revenue level, and what happens if filings lapse. Source: IRS.
- [ ] **Texas Comptroller tax exemption:** state-level exemption applications (franchise tax, sales tax) — separate from federal status and not automatic. Source: Texas Comptroller.
- [ ] **Charitable fundraising considerations:** Texas registration/solicitation rules if donations are solicited, including any thresholds and disclosures. Verify with the Secretary of State and, if needed, counsel.
- [ ] **Insurance:** general liability, event coverage, directors & officers (if a board forms), volunteer accident coverage. Obtain quotes; confirm what host venues already cover.
- [ ] **Volunteer liability:** protections and limits for volunteers under Texas and federal law; what requires waivers, background checks (especially if youth programs ever launch), or supervision ratios.
- [ ] **Intellectual property:** confirm the future license choice covers curricula, brand assets, and volunteer-contributed materials; contributor agreement if contributions become significant.
- [ ] **Minor participant protections:** if youth programming is ever approved — parental consent, supervision, provider age minimums, school policies, background checks. See [participant-safeguarding.md](../safety/participant-safeguarding.md). Not active today.
- [ ] **Employment vs. volunteer classification:** if anyone is ever paid — payroll tax, workers' comp, and reporting obligations. Verify before any payment.
- [ ] **Record retention:** how long to keep financial, governance, and program records. Confirm with accountant/attorney if formalizing.
