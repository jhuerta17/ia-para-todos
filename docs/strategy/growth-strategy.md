# Growth Strategy

> **DRAFT:** Disciplined expansion doctrine. Proposed phases with proposed graduation criteria — nothing approved, nothing scheduled. The default answer to "should we expand?" is "not until the current phase's criteria are met."

## Core rule

**Prove repeatability, then grow.** Skipping phases is how initiatives collapse under their own ambition. Each phase below must earn the next with evidence.

## Proposed phases

### Phase 0 — Validate (proposed)

- Host conversations, ~10 participant interviews, draft Workshop 1.
- Graduate when: a venue is secured, audience needs are documented, and readiness requirements are known.

### Phase 1 — Pilot (proposed)

- ~2 small workshops (10–20 participants each), 1+ assistant facilitator, anonymous feedback.
- Graduate when: beginners can complete the exercises, confidence improves, real costs are known, and the curriculum has been revised at least once based on evidence.

### Phase 2 — Repeat (proposed)

- Same workshops, second venue, second lead facilitator (not the founder).
- Graduate when: a non-founder facilitator delivers successfully using only written materials, and demand sustains without extraordinary effort.

### Phase 3 — Extend (proposed)

- Full workshop series running; libraries/centers added; volunteer pipeline functioning.
- Revisit the formal-structure question (fiscal sponsorship vs. independent entity) based on evidence.

### Phase 4 — Scale the model (proposed)

- Additional venue types, possible English track, facilitator training cohorts.
- Only with sustainable funding and governance in place.

Detailed sequencing will live under `../roadmap/` (planned) once drafted.

## Intentional growth constraints

- **One new venue type at a time.** Churches → libraries/centers → schools → workforce. Each has different logistics, safeguarding, and partnership dynamics.
- **Facilitators before workshops.** Never schedule what the trained volunteer bench can't deliver.
- **Curriculum before marketing.** Outreach promises only what tested materials can deliver.
- **Youth programming last.** Requires safeguarding infrastructure that doesn't exist yet.

## Anti-patterns we refuse

- Announcing a multi-city vision before one successful pilot.
- Adding advanced workshops before introductory ones are validated.
- Accepting funding that requires scale we haven't earned.
- Measuring growth in announcements instead of completed exercises and retained learners.
