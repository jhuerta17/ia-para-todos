# Partnerships

> **DRAFT:** Partnership approach for pilot and beyond. No partnerships are confirmed, sought formally, or committed.

## Why partners matter

Trusted local organizations provide what we cannot build quickly: community trust, venues, outreach channels, and cultural guidance. We provide curricula, facilitators, and materials. Neither side succeeds alone.

## Venue progression (proposed)

1. **Pilot:** Hispanic churches in Dallas–Fort Worth (trusted, accessible, available space).
2. **Near-term:** public libraries, community centers.
3. **Later:** schools, nonprofits, workforce development programs, other accessible venues.

Each stage must prove repeatability before the next begins (see [growth-strategy.md](growth-strategy.md) and [milestones](../roadmap/milestones.md)).

## Partnership principles

- **Mission alignment:** partners support free, inclusive, nonsectarian education.
- **No strings:** partners don't dictate curriculum content, attendee requirements (beyond safety), or messaging.
- **Mutual clarity:** venue, insurance, safety, outreach, and equipment responsibilities agreed in writing before each workshop (see [venue-requirements.md](../operations/venue-requirements.md)).
- **Respect for hosts:** we adapt scheduling, language, and logistics to the community.
- **Nonsectarian delivery:** workshops contain no religious content regardless of venue; participation never requires religious affiliation.
- **No commercial use:** partner relationships and attendee access are never used for marketing or lead generation by either side.

## What we ask of hosts

- Accessible space with seating, power, and restrooms.
- Help announcing through existing channels (bulletins, chats, announcements).
- A point of contact present during the workshop.
- Honest feedback afterward.

## What hosts receive

- Free workshops for their community, delivered by trained facilitators.
- Printed take-home materials for participants.
- Anonymous, aggregate outcome summaries (never individual data).
- Public acknowledgment only with explicit consent.

## Funder and sponsor relationships (future)

If funding materializes: written agreements stating no content influence, no data sharing, and no exclusivity; acknowledgment wording approved by both sides; right to decline or return funds that develop strings. See [sustainability.md](sustainability.md).
