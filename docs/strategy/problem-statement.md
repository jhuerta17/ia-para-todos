# Problem Statement

> **DRAFT:** Working problem framing. To be validated through audience interviews before curricula are finalized.

## The problem

Artificial intelligence is increasingly embedded in education, employment, business, and everyday life — yet access to understanding it is deeply uneven. Adults with limited exposure to technology, especially non-English-speaking community members, risk falling further behind as AI reshapes the tools their children use at school, their employers expect at work, and their communities rely on for information.

## Who experiences it most acutely

- **Parents** who see their children using AI tools they don't understand and can't guide.
- **Spanish-speaking adults** for whom most AI education, interfaces, and safety guidance default to English.
- **Older adults and workers** whose jobs and daily tasks are changing without accessible retraining.
- **Small-business owners** who could benefit from AI assistance but don't know where to start safely.
- **Community members with limited digital literacy** for whom even account creation and app navigation are barriers.

See [../organization/target-audience.md](../organization/target-audience.md) for the full audience picture.

## Why existing options don't close the gap

- Most AI tutorials assume English fluency, laptops, and baseline digital skills.
- Online self-study demands exactly the navigation skills beginners lack.
- Paid courses and tools exclude the people who would benefit most.
- Generic "tech talks" inform but don't build hands-on capability.
- Hype-heavy coverage obscures AI's real limits, feeding either blind trust or total avoidance.

## The gap we aim to address

**Free, in-person, Spanish-first, smartphone-friendly, hands-on AI education** — delivered through trusted community venues, teaching practical skills alongside accuracy, privacy, and verification habits.

## What success would look like

Not attendance counts, but changed capability: participants who complete a practical AI task in a workshop, report higher confidence afterward, and report using AI again — carefully — weeks later. Measurement approaches will be defined under `../operations/` (planned) and piloted before being treated as benchmarks.

## Deliberately out of scope

- Advanced technical training (coding, ML engineering).
- Youth education as an initial offering — a future program area with separate safeguarding requirements.
- Policy advocacy on AI regulation — we would teach responsible use, not lobby.
