# Sustainability

> **DRAFT:** Early thinking only. No funding sought, offered, or committed. Validate the program first; fund the proven model second.

## Principle

Financial sustainability serves the mission — never the reverse. We will not chase funding that distorts the program (paid tools, vendor favoritism, exclusionary eligibility, mission drift, data sharing).

## Cost reality

Free to participants does not mean free to run. The pilot exists partly to measure true costs. Categories live in [budget-template.md](../operations/budget-template.md): materials, transport, supplies, insurance, software, equipment, volunteer support, accessibility, administration.

**Pilot goal:** know the real cost per workshop and cost per participant by Day 90 — from receipts, not estimates.

## Possible future funding models (none committed)

- **Individual donations** — small community gifts once a legal way to receive them exists (fiscal sponsor or entity). Most mission-aligned if unrestricted.
- **Local business sponsorships** — neighborhood businesses supporting workshops; no curriculum influence, no lead sharing, acknowledgment only with clear boundaries.
- **Community grants** — local foundations and civic funds aligned with digital literacy and equity; pursued only when eligible and when reporting burden fits capacity.
- **Technology-company support** — device donations, connectivity, or community grants; accepted only without product endorsement requirements or data access.
- **In-kind venue and equipment support** — hosts providing rooms, projectors, printing; the backbone of the pilot phase. Track fair value privately for honest cost reporting.
- **Fiscal sponsorship** — the likely bridge: receive tax-deductible funds through a sponsor's umbrella without forming a new entity (see [formation-options.md](../nonprofit/formation-options.md)).

## Staged approach

1. **Pilot (now):** near-zero fixed costs, out-of-pocket, in-kind venues. Track everything.
2. **Validation:** small, simple money only after demand is proven; explore fiscal sponsorship.
3. **Growth:** diversified support only if scale is earned — grants, recurring small donors, in-kind partnerships. Paid coordination only when volunteer capacity is the proven bottleneck.

## What we will not do for money

- Charge participants for core workshops or require paid tools.
- Share participant data with funders (we don't hold it — see [privacy-guidelines.md](../safety/privacy-guidelines.md)).
- Accept funding conditioned on religious, political, or commercial messaging.
- Use the initiative for lead generation for any business.
- Inflate impact numbers to satisfy a grant report.
