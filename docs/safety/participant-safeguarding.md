# Participant Safeguarding

> **DRAFT:** Proposed safeguarding standards. Adult provisions apply from the first workshop; youth provisions are planning-only — **no youth programs are approved or active**, and none may launch until the requirements below are met.

## Scope

Everyone who attends, volunteers at, or hosts our activities deserves physical, emotional, and informational safety. Safeguarding scales with vulnerability: standard care for adults, heightened requirements for anyone under 18.

## Safeguarding adults (applies now)

- **Welcoming environment:** respectful facilitation; no mocking questions; no pressure to share personal matters (immigration status, income, health, beliefs).
- **Physical safety:** venue walkthrough covers exits, first aid, lighting, and accessibility (see [venue-requirements.md](../operations/venue-requirements.md)); incidents stop the session first, paperwork later.
- **Informational safety:** fictional exercise data; no photos of participants without explicit consent (prefer none); anonymous feedback; private storage of any operational records.
- **Boundaries:** volunteers don't exchange personal contacts for non-project purposes, meet participants alone to "help extra," or provide professional advice (legal/medical/financial) — they refer to qualified help.
- **Reporting:** concerns about safety or conduct go to the lead facilitator promptly and confidentially; serious concerns pause the involved volunteer's participation pending review. (A formal reporting channel arrives in a later setup phase.)

## Youth under 18 — future programs only (NOT active)

Youth programming is a possible future area. It remains **unapproved** until all of the following exist:

- **Parental consent:** written, informed, specific to the activity; opt-in, never assumed; parents informed about AI tools used, data practices, and supervision.
- **Supervision:** minimum two unrelated vetted adults present at all times; no one-on-one contact (in person or digital) between volunteers and minors; defined pickup/departure procedures with host venues.
- **Provider age requirements:** AI tools have their own minimum ages and terms — verify each tool's current terms before any youth use; underage participants must not be routed around age gates, and activities must be redesigned if tools are age-restricted.
- **Volunteer vetting:** background checks and reference checks for all youth-facing volunteers, per legal requirements and host policies; safeguarding training before contact.
- **School policies:** when working with schools, follow the school's safeguarding, consent, photography, and data policies as the floor — ours add on top, never under.
- **Content boundaries:** no personal data from minors in any exercise; no accounts created for minors by volunteers; no direct messaging between volunteers and minors; photography rules stricter than adults (default: none).
- **Incident response:** documented escalation path (lead → organizer → parents → authorities/host as required), with mandatory reporting obligations verified for Texas.

Until these are built, staffed, and documented: **adults only**, and Workshop 4's family content addresses parents — not children directly.

## Data-specific protections

Covered in [privacy-guidelines.md](privacy-guidelines.md); youth data (whenever relevant) gets the strictest handling: collect nothing identifiable by default, retain nothing beyond the session, and disclose practices to parents up front.
